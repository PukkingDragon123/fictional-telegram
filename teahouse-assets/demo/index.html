Old Rundown Teahouse
Drag props around. Drag empty space (or arrow keys / A D) to pan between the four rooms.

Window layers scroll at their own parallax speed and tile horizontally. The fire spirit (try the fire button), the alien cat, the cloth (curtains, noren, quilt, cloak, scarf, towel), lanterns, lamp, candles, incense, steam, trees and flowers are all looping sprite sheets.

The Bear Must Eat - promo video + trailer: credits and licenses
================================================================

Files
  the-bear-must-eat-promo.mp4     promo: the villagers explain the game in their own speech bubbles
  the-bear-must-eat-trailer.mp4   trailer: sunrise, Reynard wakes up, a short montage, title

Picture: 100% real in-game footage of The Bear Must Eat (its own renderer, rigs, speech
bubbles, fonts), captured frame by frame with tools/video/ (render.mjs + director.js +
shots_promo.js / shots_trailer.js) and cut with tools/video/build.py.

Game sound effects and the villagers' "voices" (babble): the game's own procedural synth
(src/audio/audio.js), rendered offline from the logged in-game events (tools/video/sfx.mjs).

Why not myinstants.com: most clips there are unlicensed rips of TV shows, films and songs
(no license is given for them), which can get an uploaded trailer muted or taken down. The
meme-style sounds above (boom, air horn, record scratch, slide whistle, sad trombone, crowd
gasp, ...) are free-licensed (CC0) equivalents.

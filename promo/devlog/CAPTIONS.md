# Devlog posts: captions + hashtags

One video a day, same time each day (evenings work well). Upload the MP4 as-is (1080x1920, 30 fps,
-14 LUFS). The hook text is already burned into the first frames, so pick an early frame as the cover.
All music and sound effects are the game's own soundtrack/synth, so there's nothing to license — post
with the video's original sound. On TikTok keep 3–5 hashtags; on Instagram you can use the longer set.

**TikTok hashtags:** `#gamedev #indiedev #devlog #cozygames #pixelart`

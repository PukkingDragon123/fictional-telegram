Bundled pixel fonts
===================

m6x11plus.woff2   (title / numbers font, --font-title)
m5x7.woff2        (body / dialogue font,  --font-body)

Both fonts by Daniel Linssen (managore)
  https://managore.itch.io/m6x11
  https://managore.itch.io/m5x7

Licence (from the author's pages):
  m6x11 / m6x11plus: "free to use with attribution"
  m5x7:              "free to use but attribution appreciated"
  Commercial and non-commercial use permitted.

Required credit (put this in the game's credits screen):
  "m6x11plus and m5x7 fonts by Daniel Linssen (managore.itch.io)"

The files were converted losslessly from the original .ttf downloads to
.woff2 (fontTools); glyph outlines are unchanged.
